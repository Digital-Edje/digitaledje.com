Security

Your data never leaves your facility.

Most AI tools send your documents to someone else's cloud. The QE Agent does the opposite. The AI model runs on the device, on your network, under your control. Your procedures, inspection records and customer drawings stay where they are today.

FAQ

Questions quality leaders ask first.

Does any of our data go to the cloud?

No. The AI model runs on the Mac mini at your site. Your documents and your conversations with the agent stay on that device, on your network.

Can it run on a network with no internet access?

Yes. After setup the agent works fully offline. We deliver updates as packages your team reviews and installs.

We're ITAR-registered. Can we use it?

Yes. The QE Agent is scoped to quality system documents, not export-controlled technical data such as drawings or technical specifications. Your export compliance officer decides which folders get connected, and folders with controlled technical data stay out of scope. Because nothing leaves your facility, it also fits the data-handling controls you already have for customer and Nadcap requirements.

Does it replace our quality engineers?

No. It takes on the reading, drafting and checking so your engineers spend their time on judgment calls. Every finding and draft is reviewed and approved by your people.

Which standards does it work with?

AS9100 and ISO 9001 today, with support for planning your move to IA9100. Your licensed copies of the standards stay with you; the agent works from your own QMS documents.

How is it priced?

By subscription, based on your site size and the agents you need. The Mac mini, setup, updates and support are included, and the hardware stays ours so you never have to replace it yourself. Request a demo for a quote.
